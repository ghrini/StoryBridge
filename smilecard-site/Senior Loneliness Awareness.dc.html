Why it matters

Loneliness in Seniors is a raising concern in the current society. It poses several risks.

Together we can work towards reducing this loneliness for our loved ones and all the seniors in our lives and everywhere.

I would like to take this opportunity to raise the awareness on senior loneliness in the community and help community to connect with seniors and keep in touch with everyone.
