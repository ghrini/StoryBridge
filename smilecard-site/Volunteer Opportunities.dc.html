How to submit your cards

Step 1

Take a picture or scan

Take a picture/scan that shows your card clearly and vertically. You can use a scanning app or the camera in the notes app (iPhone only).

Step 2

Pick a category

Make sure the card fits into one of the categories listed in the google form, if it is a category that isn't listed, click other and write your theme.

Valentine's Day Christmas St. Patricks Religious themes Plain drawing (no words) Other
Step 3

Check and send

Make sure all the information in the form is filled and that your cards picture quality is good. Press submit at the end.

Anyone that submits more than 25 cards will be recognized as a star contributor!
