How to submit cards

Make a card, send a smile

  1. 1 Make your cardHandmade, digital, a letter: any theme that brings a smile.
  2. 2 Take a picture or scanShow the whole card clearly and upright. A scanning app or the camera in the iPhone Notes app works well.
  3. 3 Fill out the formPick the categories that fit your cards, fill in every field, then press Submit.
