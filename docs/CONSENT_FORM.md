# StoryBridge consent form (template)

_Template drafted with AI help. **Review it with a trusted adult (a parent, teacher, or SmileCard advisor) before using it, and with the care facility if the storyteller lives in one.** Read it aloud with the storyteller and answer their questions. Only ask people who can understand and decide for themselves. If someone may not be able to give informed consent (for example because of significant memory loss), do not record them for this project._

---

**Project:** StoryBridge, a SmileCard project by [student name]
**Storyteller:** ______________________ **Date:** ____________

## What we would like to do
We would like to record you telling a few memories, about 5 to 10 minutes, and turn them into a short storybook for you and your family.

## What happens to your recording
- The recording is kept **only on one computer**. It is not put on the internet.
- The computer turns your words into written text.
- **Only if you say yes below:** the written text (never your voice) is sent to Google's Gemini AI service, which helps write the story and make the changes your family asks for. On free Google accounts, Google may keep and use what it receives, and people there may read it.
- A person reads the whole story with you or your family and confirms it before anything is printed or shared.
- **Only if you say yes below:** the book has codes that play short parts of your recording. They only work on the same Wi-Fi as the StoryBridge computer, while it is running.
- We will leave out health details, addresses, money matters, and other people's private information.

## You are in charge
- You can stop at any time, skip any question, or say "please don't include that."
- You can change your mind later. If you do, we delete the recording, the text, the photos, and the book.
- Nothing is shared with anyone outside your family without your permission.
